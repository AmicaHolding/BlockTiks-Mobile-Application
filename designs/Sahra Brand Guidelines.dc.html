01 · The name
سهرة
sah·ra — the evening out

The hours after sunset when people gather for music, theatre, football, comedy, dinner or simply each other. Every Arabic speaker knows the word, from Casablanca to Muscat, and it carries warmth rather than formality.

Sahra sells tickets, but what people are really buying is the night itself.

02 · Philosophy
The ticket is a promise.
Paying for a ticket starts the anticipation. Our design should feel like holding a real ticket: tactile, warm and worth keeping.
Arabic first, never Arabic-flavoured.
The identity is built from real letterforms, not borrowed decoration. It is cultural without being religious and regional without being touristic.
Get out of the way.
Ticketing is stressful, so the interface stays calm, high-contrast and fast. The personality lives in the logo, colour and copy.
03 · Logo
A ticket stub carrying the word سهرة in Ruqaa calligraphy, like the name printed on an admission ticket. There is no separate symbol: the word is the mark.
Primary. Gold ticket on Night or dark imagery.
Night. Night ticket on Sand, white or light imagery.
App icons. Primary, plus an alternate with the calligraphy only.
Clear space = notch radius (≈10% of the ticket width) on every side.
Minimum size is 48px / 12mm wide. Below that, use the alternate icon.
Don't
Put the gold ticket on Sand
Stretch or distort
Rotate
Recolour outside the palette
Also don't: add crescents, domes, lanterns or other religious and architectural motifs; retypeset سهرة in another font; add perforations, barcodes or shadows.
