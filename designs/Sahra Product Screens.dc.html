Standing zones skip the seat picker and go straight to a quantity picker. Events without a layout keep screen 03.

Fan app · buying & selling resale
There's no Resale tab. Buying: resale tickets sit in the normal ticket list on every event (03), and "Set your price" (24) covers sold-out tiers. Selling: it starts from a ticket in Tickets (06a). Choose which ones (21), set a price and how they can be bought (22), then watch it sell ticket by ticket (23). Selling, holding and waiting all appear on the same event card, because people think in events, not in buyer and seller roles.
