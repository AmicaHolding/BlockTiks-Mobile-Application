The stretched line becomes the tear line
Arabic calligraphers stretch words with the kashida (ـ). Here the stretch is drawn as a ticket’s tear line, so the logo is both the word and a ticket. You can stretch it to any width for banners, wristbands and ticket stubs. A dashed line alone works as the brand mark.

The two dots of ة, as two seats
Calligraphers draw letter dots (nuqat) as diamonds made by the pen’s nib. The two dots over the final ة become the mark: two seats side by side, split by a tear line. It works as a repeating pattern on tickets, wristbands and merch, and as a loading animation as the dots pulse.
